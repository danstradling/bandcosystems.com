Behind BANDCO Systems

I'm Dan Stradling, the founder and hands-on engineer behind BANDCO Systems. With extensive experience architecting enterprise Microsoft endpoint technologies, cloud solutions, and IT management systems, I founded BANDCO Systems to bring direct, jargon-free technical execution straight to businesses and individuals across Kent.

My engineering background bridges both digital infrastructure and physical manufacturing. Whether modelling custom automotive components and IT organisers in parametric CAD, fabricating high-precision functional 3D prints, or designing low-latency AV and Pioneer DJ streaming setups, every project is handled directly by me with zero ticket escalation or account manager delays.

When you work with BANDCO Systems, you work directly with the person designing the model, configuring the hardware, or standing behind the soundboard.
